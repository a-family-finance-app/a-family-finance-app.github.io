FamilyFinance

An expense tracker for Android that builds your expense record from the bank, card and UPI alerts on your phone, and keeps it in a spreadsheet you own: an .xlsx file on your phone or a Google Sheet in your own Drive.

  • Bank SMS is read on your phone only and never sent to the developer. SMS reading is optional and off until you turn it on.
  • A tab per month, with daily totals, top categories and how each month compares with the last.
  • Also tracks investments (FDs, RDs, mutual funds, stocks, bonds, insurance) and loans.
  • No FamilyFinance account and no servers: the developer never sees your data.
